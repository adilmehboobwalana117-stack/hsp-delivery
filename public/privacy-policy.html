Official Privacy & Data Safety Policy

Privacy Policy for HSP delivery

Effective Date: November 2026 · Service Area: Hasilpur, Pakistan · App Package: hspdelivery.com

1. Overview

HSP delivery ("we", "our", or "us") operates the HSP delivery web and Android mobile application for ordering food, groceries, pharmacy items, bakery goods, and local store deliveries. We respect your privacy and collect only the information needed to accept, dispatch, route, verify, and deliver your orders safely.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Name, email address (when signing in with Google), account role (Customer, Rider, Business Partner, or Operations Staff), and mobile phone number.
  • Order & Delivery Information: Ordered items, store name, delivery address, optional delivery instructions, payment method (Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Card, or WhatsApp Order), and payment reference IDs.
  • Location Data (GPS): Precise device coordinates are requested only when a customer pins a delivery address, a business pins its store location, or an assigned rider enables live delivery navigation after picking up an order.
  • Camera & Photo Uploads: Used only when a customer attaches a photo for a WhatsApp custom order, a business uploads product/store photos, or a rider captures a timestamped delivery proof photo at order drop-off.
  • Device Notifications: Browser/device push notification permissions used solely to alert customers, business partners, and riders about active orders and status updates.

3. How We Use & Protect Your Data

  • Calculate distance-based delivery fees and route riders accurately to your door.
  • Coordinate real-time order updates between customers, local businesses, and riders.
  • Enable one-tap phone contact (tel:) between assigned riders and customers strictly for completing an active delivery.
  • Verify prepaid and WhatsApp deliveries and resolve customer support inquiries.

Strict Privacy Isolation: Customer mobile numbers are hidden from business owners and shared only with the assigned HSP rider and operations team for delivery coordination. Rider delivery proof photos are stored in an Admin-only verification database and are never shown to customers, other riders, or business owners.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell or rent your personal data to third-party advertisers. To operate the app reliably, we use trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Secure user authentication, encrypted data transit (HTTPS/TLS), and role-based access rules.
  • Google Maps Platform: Interactive delivery maps, address pinning, and route distance calculation.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Order records and operational logs are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill deliveries, process refunds/payouts, and comply with legal or accounting obligations.

Request Account or Data Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your account profile, phone number, or saved addresses at any time by contacting HSP delivery Support via email or WhatsApp below. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

HSP delivery is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
